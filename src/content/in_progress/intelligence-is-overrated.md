I'm not stating this because I got the short end of the stick and landed within one standard deviation below the mean on a bell shaped distribution curve. A hyperintellectual will tell you that i'm stating this simply because I didn't win the intelligence lottery. But my dogmatic truth is that intelligence is overrated: nearly anyone can be great, yet most people use lack of "intellectual talent" as an excuse for lack of resilience, grit, and courage.
